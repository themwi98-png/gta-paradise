GTA PARADISE
============

Burnout Paradise City in GTA V story mode, with Burnout-style crashes,
boost and driving.

This download contains no files from either game. The setup builds Paradise
City on your PC from your own copies of the games.


WHAT YOU NEED
-------------

1. GTA V Legacy (PC). Not the Enhanced edition: the setup never touches it,
   and GTA Online is never affected. Story mode only.
2. Burnout Paradise Remastered (PC).
3. ScriptHookV, installed into GTA V:
   http://www.dev-c.com/gtav/scripthookv/
4. OpenIV, with its "mods" folder set up (in OpenIV: Tools > ASI Manager >
   install OpenIV.ASI):
   https://openiv.com/
5. CodeWalker 3.0 (dev46 or newer). Download it and unzip it anywhere,
   for example in your Downloads folder:
   https://www.gta5-mods.com/tools/codewalker-gtav-interactive-3d-map
6. About 10 GB of free disk space (the setup's working files, plus the
   GTA mods folder).


INSTALLING
----------

1. Close GTA V.
2. Unzip this folder anywhere and run paradise-setup.exe.
   Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" the first time: the setup
   isn't digitally signed (signing costs money; most free mods aren't).
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. It finds your games and CodeWalker by itself. If one isn't found (or the
   wrong one is), type its folder or press Browse... to pick it. Green ticks
   mean everything is ready.
4. Press Install. The first install takes about 15-20 minutes: it converts
   the whole city. Later installs (after an update) only redo what changed.
5. Start GTA V and load story mode.


UPDATING
--------

Download the new version, unzip it, and run its paradise-setup.exe. Your
settings in paradise.ini (in the GTA V folder) are kept.


SETTINGS
--------

paradise.ini in your GTA V folder. It is re-read every few seconds, so you
can change it while playing.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------

The setup's log is at:
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\GTA-Paradise\work\out\setup.log
